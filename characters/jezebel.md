## Who Jezebel is

Jezebel is queen of Israel in the mid-9th century BC. She appears in 1 Kings 16–21 and 2 Kings 9 (28 of her 31 links), and in three verses of Revelation 2 where her name is used for a false prophetess. All the Kings verses show her as the driving force behind Ahab's sins.

## What the verses tell us

**Marriage and religion.** "as if it had been a light thing for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, that he took to wife Jezebel the daughter of Ethbaal king of the Zidonians, and went and served Baal, and worshipped him" (1 Kings 16:31). When "Jezebel cut off the prophets of the LORD", Obadiah hid a hundred in caves and fed them (18:4, 18:13). The 450 prophets of Baal and 400 of the groves "eat at Jezebel's table" (18:19).

**Against Elijah.** After Carmel, Ahab tells her all Elijah had done "and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword" (19:1). She sends a messenger to Elijah: "So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time" (19:2), and he flees to Horeb (see [Elijah](elijah.md)).

**Naboth's vineyard.** Ahab sulks because Naboth will not sell his vineyard. "Jezebel his wife came to him, and said unto him, Why is thy spirit so sad, that thou eatest no bread?" (21:5). When he tells her, she says "Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel? arise, and eat bread, and let thine heart be merry: I will give thee the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite" (21:7). "She wrote letters in Ahab's name, and sealed them with his seal" to the elders and nobles of Naboth's city (21:8), ordering "Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people" (21:9), with two false witnesses to say that Naboth blasphemed God and the king (21:10). The elders do "as Jezebel had sent unto them" (21:11) and report "Naboth is stoned, and is dead" (21:14). She tells Ahab to take possession (21:15). Elijah's word for her: "The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel" (21:23). The narrator says of Ahab "there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the LORD, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up" (21:25). Her son Ahaziah "walked in the way of his father, and in the way of his mother" (22:52).

**Jehu's commission.** The young prophet tells Jehu "thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master, that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets… at the hand of Jezebel" and "the dogs shall eat Jezebel in the portion of Jezreel, and there shall be none to bury her" (2 Kings 9:7, 9:10). When Joram asks "Is it peace, Jehu?", Jehu answers "What peace, so long as the whoredoms of thy mother Jezebel and her witchcrafts are so many?" (9:22).

**Her end.** "When Jehu was come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; and she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window" (9:30). She calls to him "Had Zimri peace, who slew his master?" (9:31). Jehu asks "Who is on my side?" and two or three eunuchs look out; he says "Throw her down" and "some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot" (9:32–33). He eats and drinks, then orders "Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king's daughter" (9:34). They find only "the skull, and the feet, and the palms of her hands" (9:35), and Jehu recalls Elijah's word: "the carcase of Jezebel shall be as dung upon the face of the field… so that they shall not say, This is Jezebel" (9:36–37).

**Revelation 2:20–22.** The risen Christ tells the church at Thyatira: "thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols. And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not… Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation" (2:20–22). This is a woman in the first-century church named after the queen, not the queen herself.

## Relationships

- **Ahab** (husband) and **Ethbaal** (father, king of the Zidonians). Children: **Ahaziah**, **Joram**, **Athaliah**, and (per TIPNR) **Joash**.
- **[Elijah](elijah.md)**: her enemy (5 shared verses). **Naboth**, **Obadiah**, **Jehu**.
- **[Jesus](jesus.md)** shares three verses with her (Revelation 2:20–22, where he is the speaker).

## Reading the data

- **2 Kings 11:16** ("they laid hands on her… and there was she slain") was linked at 0.78 but is Queen **Athaliah**, her daughter, and has been removed from the database.
- **Revelation 2:20–22** is a different woman, with Jezebel's name as a label.
- Naboth verses where the letters and stoning are described without her name (1 Kings 21:10, 21:13) are correctly below the threshold.
