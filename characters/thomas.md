## Who Thomas is

Thomas is named in the lists of the twelve (Matthew 10:3, Mark 3:18, Luke 6:15, Acts 1:13) and in three scenes in John's Gospel (11:16, 14:5, 20:24–29), plus John 21:2. The classifier links 21 verses, of which 11 are in John. Nothing else is recorded of his life in the Bible.

## What the verses tell us

**In the lists.** "Philip, and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the publican" (Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:15). Group verses about "the twelve" at the last supper (Matthew 26:20–21; Luke 22:14) include him but do not name him. In the upper room after the ascension he is one of those who "abode both Peter, and James, and John, and Andrew, Philip, and Thomas" (Acts 1:13), and they "continued with one accord in prayer and supplication" (1:14).

**"Let us also go, that we may die with him."** When Jesus decides to return to Judaea to Lazarus (John 11:7–16), "Then said Thomas, which is called Didymus, unto his fellowdisciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him" (11:16). The verse shows both his loyalty and his pessimism.

**"How can we know the way?"** At the last supper Jesus says he is going to prepare a place, and "Thomas saith unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?" (14:5). Jesus' answer: "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me" (14:6).

**Doubt and confession.** After the resurrection "Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came" (20:24). The others say "We have seen the LORD", and he answers "Except I shall see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into his side, I will not believe" (20:25). "After eight days again his disciples were within, and Thomas with them: then came Jesus, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said, Peace be unto you. Then saith he to Thomas, Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side: and be not faithless, but believing" (20:26–27). Thomas: "My LORD and my God" (20:28). Jesus: "Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed" (20:29). The text does not say Thomas actually touched the wounds.

**Galilee.** In the last chapter "there were together Simon Peter, and Thomas called Didymus, and Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two other of his disciples" (21:2), and Peter announces "I go a fishing" and they follow him (21:3).

**Revelation.** Revelation 21:14, "the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb" is linked at 0.51 as a general reference.
