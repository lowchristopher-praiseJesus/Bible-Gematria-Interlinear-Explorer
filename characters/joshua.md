## Who Joshua is

Joshua son of Nun (originally Hoshea/Oshea) appears in 309 verses: 256 of them in the book that bears his name, and most of the rest in Exodus and Numbers where he is Moses' "minister". This profile is Joshua the leader; the Zechariah 3 high priest Joshua/Jeshua is a different person and was deliberately removed from these links.

## What the verses tell us

**Moses' aide.** His first appearance is as a war leader: "Moses said unto Joshua, Choose us out men, and go out, fight with Amalek… So Joshua did as Moses had said to him" (Exodus 17:9–10). He goes up the mountain with Moses ("his minister Joshua", 24:13), hears the noise of the golden calf and says "There is a noise of war in the camp" (32:17), and stays in the tabernacle when Moses leaves it: "his servant Joshua, the son of Nun, a young man, departed not out of the tabernacle" (33:11). When two men prophesy in the camp he says "My lord Moses, forbid them", and Moses replies "Enviest thou for my sake? would God that all the LORD's people were prophets" (Numbers 11:28–29).

**The spies.** "Of the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the son of Nun… Moses called Oshea the son of Nun Jehoshua" (Numbers 13:8, 13:16). After the reconnaissance "Joshua the son of Nun, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh… rent their clothes" (14:6), urging "The land… is an exceeding good land… rebel not ye against the LORD, neither fear ye the people of the land" (14:7–9). God's verdict: none of that generation will enter "save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun" (14:30, 26:65) because "they have wholly followed the LORD" (32:12).

**Chosen successor.** "Take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lay thine hand upon him" (27:18); Moses sets him before Eleazar and the congregation (27:22). Deuteronomy repeats "Joshua the son of Nun, which standeth before thee, he shall go in thither: encourage him" (1:38), "charge Joshua, and encourage him, and strengthen him" (3:28), and Moses says publicly "Be strong and of a good courage" (31:7). The LORD calls him to the tabernacle for the charge (31:14). "Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom; for Moses had laid his hands upon him" (34:9).

**Command at the Jordan.** "Moses my servant is dead; now therefore arise, go over this Jordan" (Joshua 1:1–2). The LORD promises "as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee" (1:5) and commands "Be strong and of a good courage… this book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night… Have not I commanded thee?… the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest" (1:6–9). He sends two spies to Jericho (2:1; see [Rahab](rahab.md)), then "Sanctify yourselves: for to morrow the LORD will do wonders among you" (3:5). The priests with the ark stand in the river and "all the Israelites passed over on dry ground" (3:17). Twelve stones are taken from the Jordan (4:4–5). At Gilgal the LORD says "circumcise again the children of Israel" (5:2). Near Jericho a man with a drawn sword says "as captain of the host of the LORD am I now come. And Joshua fell on his face… Loose thy shoe from off thy foot; for the place whereon thou standest is holy" (5:13–15).

**Jericho and Ai.** God: "See, I have given into thine hand Jericho" (6:2); Joshua has seven priests carry seven trumpets before the ark (6:6). "The wall fell down flat" (6:20). At Ai, Joshua tears his clothes and falls on his face after a defeat: "Alas, O LORD God, wherefore hast thou at all brought this people over Jordan…?" (7:6–7). The LORD says "Get thee up; wherefore liest thou thus upon thy face?" (7:10); Achan confesses to hiding a Babylonian garment, silver and gold (7:20–21). Then "Fear not, neither be thou dismayed… go up to Ai" (8:1) and an ambush wins the city (8:3–9). Israelite leaders are tricked into a treaty with the Gibeonites (9:8, 9:15).

**The long day.** Joshua rescues Gibeon: "Sun, stand thou still upon Gibeon; and thou, Moon, in the valley of Ajalon" (10:12). "There was no day like that before it or after it, that the LORD hearkened unto the voice of a man: for the LORD fought for Israel" (10:14). Chapters 10–12 summarise the southern and northern campaigns ("the kings of the country which Joshua and the children of Israel smote", 12:7).

**Dividing the land.** "Joshua was old and stricken in years… there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed" (13:1). Joshua and Eleazar the priest distribute it by lot at Shiloh (14:1, 19:51). He blesses Caleb and gives him Hebron (14:13), tells the tribe of Joseph "thou art a great people, and hast great power" (17:17), rebukes the seven tribes: "How long are ye slack to go to possess the land?" (18:3). He himself receives Timnath-serah in Mount Ephraim (19:50). Cities of refuge (20:1–2) and cities for the Levites (21:1–2) follow; he sends the eastern tribes home with a blessing (22:6).

**Farewell.** "Joshua waxed old and stricken in age" and calls all Israel (23:1–2). At Shechem (24:1) he tells the story from Abraham on and gives the challenge: "choose you this day whom ye will serve… but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD" (24:15). To the people's oath he says "Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God" (24:19) and "Ye are witnesses against yourselves" (24:22). He dies at 110 (24:29; Judges 2:8), buried at Timnath-serah (24:30). "Israel served the LORD all the days of Joshua" (24:31).

**After him.** Judges 2:6–8 replays the dismissal of the tribes and Joshua's death. Acts 7:45 (where the KJV writes "Jesus", the Greek form of Joshua) has the fathers bringing the tabernacle "with Jesus into the possession of the Gentiles"; Hebrews 4:8 argues "if Jesus had given them rest, then would he not afterward have spoken of another day", and Hebrews 11:30 recalls that "by faith the walls of Jericho fell down".

## Relationships

- **[Moses](moses.md)**: master and predecessor (46 shared verses); succession is from Numbers 27 to Deuteronomy 34. **Nun** is his father, of the tribe of Ephraim.
- **Caleb son of Jephunneh**: fellow spy and the other survivor of that generation.
- **Eleazar** the priest (Aaron's son): partner in dividing the land.
- **[Rahab](rahab.md)**: he keeps his spies' promise to save her (6 shared verses).
- **[Aaron](aaron.md)** and **[Joseph](joseph.md)** (as "the house of Joseph", Joshua 17) appear with him in a few verses.

## Reading the data

- The Jericho narrative is thinner than expected: the collapse of the walls (Joshua 6:20) scores only 0.31, and Joshua 6:1, 6:4–5, 6:9 are under threshold because "the people" or "the priests" are the actors. The reverse holds for Joshua 1, 10 and 24, which are almost entirely linked.
- Namesakes are handled well: Joshua the high priest (Zechariah 3) is not in this list, and Jesus Christ is not confused with Joshua in Acts 7:45 and Hebrews 4:8 (the two "Jesus" verses are linked here at 0.64 and above).
- Named but not linked: Judges 2:21, Judges 1:1, 1 Kings 16:34 (Hiel and Jericho, "spake by Joshua").
