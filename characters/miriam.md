## Who Miriam is

Miriam is named in only 12 verses of the Bible, but the classifier links 20 because the unnamed "sister" of the baby Moses in Exodus 2 is understood to be her. Her three moments are the river, the sea and the tabernacle door.

## What the verses tell us

**The sister at the river (Exodus 2).** While the baby Moses lies in a basket among the reeds, "his sister stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him" (2:4). When Pharaoh's daughter opens it and says "This is one of the Hebrews' children" (2:6), the sister steps forward: "Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?" (2:7). Pharaoh's daughter says "Go", and "the maid went and called the child's mother" (2:8). The text does not name her in Exodus 2; Numbers 26:59 gives the family: Jochebed "bare unto Amram Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister".

**Prophetess and singer.** After the sea is crossed, "Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel in her hand; and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances" (15:20). She leads them in the refrain: "Sing ye to the LORD, for he hath triumphed gloriously; the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea" (15:21).

**Challenge to Moses.** "Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married" (Numbers 12:1). Their real complaint: "Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us? And the LORD heard it" (12:2). The LORD calls the three to the tabernacle, comes down in the pillar of the cloud, and calls "Aaron and Miriam" forward (12:4–5). He contrasts prophets who get visions with "my servant Moses… With him will I speak mouth to mouth… wherefore then were ye not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?" (12:6–8). "The cloud departed… and, behold, Miriam became leprous, white as snow" (12:10). Aaron pleads "Let her not be as one dead" (12:12) and Moses cries "Heal her now, O God, I beseech thee" (12:13). She is shut out of the camp seven days, "and the people journeyed not till Miriam was brought in again" (12:14–15).

**Death and memory.** "The people abode in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there" (20:1). Deuteronomy 24:9 uses her as a warning: "Remember what the LORD thy God did unto Miriam by the way, after that ye were come forth out of Egypt". 1 Chronicles 6:3 lists her with Aaron and Moses as Amram's children, and Micah 6:4 puts her alongside them as a leader God sent: "I sent before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam."

## Relationships

- **[Moses](moses.md)** and **[Aaron](aaron.md)**: brothers. She shares 11 verses with Moses and 8 with Aaron.
- **Amram** and **Jochebed**: parents (Numbers 26:59).
- **Pharaoh's daughter**: the woman she speaks to in Exodus 2.
- No husband or children are recorded.

## Reading the data

- Exodus 2:4, 2:7, 2:8 link Miriam only because "his sister" is her; the text never gives the name there. The classifier is deriving that from the context, which is the reading traditional interpreters share.
- Numbers 12:8 (0.52) and 1 Chronicles 6:3 (0.62) are the two weakest links. TIPNR lists 12 verses that name her, and every one is included in the 20, so no naming was missed.
