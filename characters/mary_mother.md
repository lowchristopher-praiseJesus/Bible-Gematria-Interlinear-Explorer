## Who Mary is

This profile is **Mary the mother of Jesus**, not Mary Magdalene, Mary of Bethany or Mary the mother of James (the classifier keeps them apart). Her story is in the Gospels' infancy narratives (Luke 1–2, Matthew 1–2, 62 verses), in the "his mother and brethren" scenes, at Cana and the cross in John, and in Acts 1:14. She is unnamed in some verses ("his mother", "the mother of Jesus") and is called "Woman" by Jesus (John 2:4, 19:26).

## What the verses tell us

**The annunciation.** The angel Gabriel is sent "to a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary" (Luke 1:27). "Hail, thou that art highly favoured, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women" (1:28). "When she saw him, she was troubled at his saying, and cast in her mind what manner of salutation this should be" (1:29). "Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favour with God. And, behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS" (1:30–31). Her question: "How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?" (1:34). The answer: "The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God" (1:35). Her reply: "Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word" (1:38).

**Visiting Elisabeth.** "Mary arose in those days, and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Juda; and entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elisabeth" (Luke 1:39–40). Elisabeth "spake out with a loud voice, and said, Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb. And whence is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?" (1:42–43). Mary answers with the song "My soul doth magnify the Lord" (1:46) and stays about three months before returning home (1:56).

**Joseph's dilemma.** Matthew tells it from Joseph's side: "When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost" (Matthew 1:18). Joseph, "being a just man, and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily" (1:19). An angel says "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost" (1:20), and he "took unto him his wife: and knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS" (1:24–25).

**Birth and childhood.** They go to Bethlehem "to be taxed with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child" (Luke 2:5). "She brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn" (2:7). The shepherds "found Mary, and Joseph, and the babe lying in a manger" (2:16), and "Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart" (2:19). At the presentation, Simeon tells Mary "this child is set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel; and for a sign which shall be spoken against; (Yea, a sword shall pierce through thy own soul also,)" (2:34–35). The magi find "the young child with Mary his mother, and fell down, and worshipped him" (Matthew 2:11). Joseph takes "the young child and his mother by night" to Egypt (2:13–14), returns to Israel after Herod's death (2:20–21) and settles in Nazareth. At twelve Jesus stays in the temple; Mary says "Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (Luke 2:48), and afterwards "his mother kept all these sayings in her heart" (2:51).

**Cana.** "The third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee; and the mother of Jesus was there" (John 2:1). "When they wanted wine, the mother of Jesus saith unto him, They have no wine" (2:3). Jesus: "Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come" (2:4). She tells the servants "Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it" (2:5). "After this he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples" (2:12).

**The family and his ministry.** "His mother and his brethren stood without, desiring to speak with him" (Matthew 12:46; Mark 3:31; Luke 8:19). Jesus: "Who is my mother? and who are my brethren?… My mother and my brethren are these which hear the word of God, and do it" (Matthew 12:48; Luke 8:21). In Nazareth people ask "Is not this the carpenter's son? is not his mother called Mary? and his brethren, James, and Joses, and Simon, and Judas?" (Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3), and in Capernaum "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?" (John 6:42).

**At the cross.** "Now there stood by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother's sister, Mary the wife of Cleophas, and Mary Magdalene. When Jesus therefore saw his mother, and the disciple standing by, whom he loved, he saith unto his mother, Woman, behold thy son! Then saith he to the disciple, Behold thy mother! And from that hour that disciple took her unto his own home" (John 19:25–27).

**After the resurrection.** "These all continued with one accord in prayer and supplication, with the women, and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brethren" (Acts 1:14), her last appearance by name.

**Other links.** Paul: "when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law" (Galatians 4:4, 0.59). Micah 5:3 ("she which travaileth hath brought forth", 0.5+) and Revelation 12:5 (the woman and her child, 0.51) are read by many interpreters as pointing to her, but the verses name no one.

## Relationships

- **[Jesus](jesus.md)**: her son (53 shared verses). **[Joseph](joseph_husband.md)**: husband (29 shared verses). Her other children are named in Matthew 13:55 as **James, Joses, Simon and Judas** (Jude), with unnamed sisters.
- **Elisabeth** and **Zacharias**: her relative and the priest, parents of **[John the Baptist](john_baptist.md)** (3 shared verses).
- **[John the apostle](john_apostle.md)**: "the disciple whom Jesus loved", who takes her home (5 shared verses: John 2:12, 19:26–27, Acts 1:14 and 2:1). **[Mary Magdalene](mary_magdalene.md)** stands with her at the cross (John 19:25). **[Peter](peter.md)** (2 shared, Acts 1:14 and 2:1) and **[Thomas](thomas.md)** (1, Acts 1:14) are in the Acts 1 company.
- **Her sister** (or sister-in-law): "Mary the wife of Cleophas" (John 19:25); TIPNR suggests Salome (uncertain).
- **Heli**: her father (TIPNR, from Luke 3:23).

## Reading the data

- The scoring is very reliable inside Luke 1–2, Matthew 1–2 and John 2 and 19. Lower scores (0.5–0.66) are for verses where "his mother" is only implied: Matthew 12:48, Mark 3:33, John 6:42, Acts 2:1 (0.51, the Pentecost gathering, which includes her by reading Acts 1:14).
- **Misidentification risk:** the marker "Mary" in Mark 15:40, 15:47 and 16:1 ("Mary the mother of James and Joses") is not linked, and Mary Magdalene and Mary of Bethany do not appear here.
- Micah 5:3, Galatians 4:4 and Revelation 12:5 are interpretive links.
