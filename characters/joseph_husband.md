## Who Joseph is

This profile is **Joseph the husband of Mary**, not Joseph son of Jacob ([Joseph](joseph.md)) or Joseph of Arimathea. He appears in the Gospels' infancy narratives (Matthew 1–2, Luke 1–2: 33 of 34 links) and is later referred to as "Joseph's son" (Luke 4:22, John 6:42). He speaks no recorded words at all.

## What the verses tell us

**Ancestry.** Matthew's genealogy ends "Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ" (Matthew 1:16). Luke calls him "a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David" (Luke 1:27) and says he went up to Bethlehem "because he was of the house and lineage of David" (2:4).

**The dilemma and the dream.** "When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost" (Matthew 1:18). "Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away privily" (1:19). An angel of the Lord appears to him in a dream: "Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins" (1:20–21). "Then Joseph being raised from sleep did as the angel of the Lord had bidden him, and took unto him his wife: and knew her not till she had brought forth her firstborn son: and he called his name JESUS" (1:24–25).

**Bethlehem.** "Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judaea, unto the city of David, which is called Bethlehem… To be taxed with Mary his espoused wife, being great with child" (Luke 2:4–5). The shepherds "came with haste, and found Mary, and Joseph, and the babe lying in a manger" (2:16).

**Presentation.** At the temple, "when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him after the custom of the law" (2:27), "Joseph and his mother marvelled at those things which were spoken of him" (2:33), and Simeon blessed them (2:34). They return "into Galilee, to their own city Nazareth" (2:39).

**Egypt and back.** After the magi leave, "the angel of the Lord appeareth to Joseph in a dream, saying, Arise, and take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and be thou there until I bring thee word: for Herod will seek the young child to destroy him. When he arose, he took the young child and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt" (Matthew 2:13–14). After Herod's death the angel appears again: "Arise, and take the young child and his mother, and go into the land of Israel: for they are dead which sought the young child's life" (2:19–21). "When he heard that Archelaus did reign in Judaea in the room of his father Herod, he was afraid to go thither: notwithstanding, being warned of God in a dream, he turned aside into the parts of Galilee: and he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth" (2:22–23).

**The twelve-year-old.** "His parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the passover" (Luke 2:41). When Jesus is twelve and stays behind, "Joseph and his mother knew not of it. But they, supposing him to have been in the company, went a day's journey" (2:43–44) and return to seek him (2:45). Mary says "thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing" (2:48); Jesus answers "wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?" (2:49). He "went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject unto them" (2:51).

**Afterwards.** Joseph does not appear by name in the ministry. People in Nazareth say "Is not this Joseph's son?" (Luke 4:22) and in Capernaum "Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know?" (John 6:42). The Gospels of Matthew and Mark call Jesus "the carpenter's son" (Matthew 13:55) and "the carpenter" (Mark 6:3), which is the only trade named in the family.

## Relationships

- **[Mary](mary_mother.md)**: wife (29 shared verses). **[Jesus](jesus.md)**: legal and presumed father of Jesus (26 shared verses); the sons **James, Joses, Simon and Judas** (Jude) named in Matthew 13:55 are counted as Joseph's children in TIPNR.
- **Jacob** (Matthew 1:16; Luke calls his father **Heli**, Luke 3:23): his father. TIPNR lists **Alphaeus** as a possible brother (uncertain).
- **Simeon**, the shepherds and the magi meet the family; **Herod** and **Archelaus** are the threats that send them to Egypt and back to Galilee.

## Reading the data

- **John 9:23** ("his parents", 0.58) was a wrong link, since they are the parents of the man born blind, and has been removed from the database. Luke 2:27, 2:34 and 2:42 (0.57–0.64) are "the parents" scenes, probably correct, but Luke 2:34 (Simeon speaking to Mary) may not name him.
- The classifier has kept this Joseph apart from the patriarch: the patriarch's 345 links never include a Gospel verse, and Joseph the husband is only in Matthew, Luke and John.
- Luke 3:23 (0.49) and John 1:45 (0.29), where Jesus is "supposed" to be Joseph's son, were missed.
