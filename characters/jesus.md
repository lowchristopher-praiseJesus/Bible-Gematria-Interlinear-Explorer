# Jesus

*Jesus of Nazareth, called the Christ: born of Mary, baptized by John, who taught, healed and called disciples in Galilee and Judaea, was crucified under Pontius Pilate, rose on the third day, and is presented in the rest of the New Testament as Lord, Saviour and coming King.*

## Who Jesus is

Jesus is by far the most-linked character in the index: 4,042 verses (about 13% of the Bible), of which 2,589 are at 0.90 or above. His links spread across the whole New Testament, and they also include 113 verses in the Old Testament that the classifier reads as speaking of him.

In the Gospels and Acts he is the subject of the narrative. In the letters and Revelation the links are mostly to verses that speak *about* him, using "Christ", "the Lord", "the Son" or "the Lamb".

## What the verses tell us

**Birth and childhood.** The angel tells Joseph "thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins" (Matthew 1:21). He is born in Bethlehem: "unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord" (Luke 2:11). The magi worship "the young child with Mary his mother" (Matthew 2:11), the family flees to Egypt and returns to Nazareth (2:13–23). At twelve he stays in the temple ("I must be about my Father's business", Luke 2:49); afterwards he "was subject unto them", and "Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man" (2:51–52; see [Mary](mary_mother.md) and [Joseph](joseph_husband.md)).

**Baptism and temptation.** "Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water: and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him… And lo a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" (Matthew 3:16–17; see [John the Baptist](john_baptist.md), 83 shared verses). "Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil. And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred" (4:1–2). To "command that these stones be made bread" he answers "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God" (4:3–4).

**Start of ministry.** "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel" (Mark 1:15). In the synagogue at Nazareth he reads "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor" and says "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears" (Luke 4:18–21). He calls fishermen: "Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men" (Matthew 4:19), gathering the twelve, among them [Peter](peter.md) (220 shared verses), [John](john_apostle.md) (169), [Thomas](thomas.md) and [Judas Iscariot](judas_iscariot.md) (82).

**Teaching.** The Sermon on the Mount opens with "Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven" (Matthew 5:3) and includes the Lord's Prayer: "Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name" (6:9). He invites "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest" (11:28). Asked for the greatest commandment: "Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart… and the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself" (22:37–39). Parable characters, such as the father in Luke 15, are correctly *not* linked to him (Luke 15:20 scores 0.06).

**Works.** He rebukes the wind and sea: "Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm" (Mark 4:39). His miracles of healing, feeding and raising the dead are throughout the four Gospels: Jairus' daughter, the loaves and fishes, walking on the sea and the raising of Lazarus ([Lazarus](lazarus.md), 24 shared verses).

**The "I am" sayings in John.** "I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger" (6:35); "I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness" (8:12); "I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep" (10:11); "I am the resurrection, and the life" (11:25); "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me" (14:6); "I am the vine, ye are the branches" (15:5). The prologue: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God" (1:1) and "the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us" (1:14). And "God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life" (3:16).

**Purpose.** "The Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many" (Matthew 20:28; Mark 10:45). "The Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost" (Luke 19:10). At Caesarea Philippi Peter confesses "Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God" (Matthew 16:16). On the mountain "Moses and Elias" appear talking with him (Matthew 17:3; [Moses](moses.md), 29 shared verses; [Elijah](elijah.md), 19).

**The last week.** He enters Jerusalem, teaches in the temple, and is anointed at Bethany. At the supper: "Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat; this is my body. And he took the cup, and gave thanks… this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins" (Matthew 26:26–28). In Gethsemane: "O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt" (26:39). [Judas](judas_iscariot.md) betrays him with a kiss (Matthew 26:47–50).

**Trial and cross.** He is taken to [Pilate](pontius_pilate.md) (69 shared verses), who three times finds "no fault in this man" and still delivers him to be crucified (Luke 23:4, 23:14, 23:22–25). "And they crucified him, and parted his garments, casting lots" (Matthew 27:35). "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do" (Luke 23:34); to the criminal beside him, "To day shalt thou be with me in paradise" (23:43); to his mother and the beloved disciple, "Woman, behold thy son… Behold thy mother!" (John 19:26–27); "Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is to say, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" (Matthew 27:46); "It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost" (John 19:30). "Jesus, when he had cried again with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost" (Matthew 27:50).

**Resurrection.** The angel to the women: "Fear not ye: for I know that ye seek Jesus, which was crucified. He is not here: for he is risen, as he said" (Matthew 28:5–6). [Mary Magdalene](mary_magdalene.md) (36 shared verses) sees him first (John 20:14–17). "Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and saith unto them, Peace be unto you… Behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself: handle me, and see; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones" (Luke 24:36–39). To [Thomas](thomas.md): "Reach hither thy finger", and Thomas answers "My LORD and my God" (John 20:27–28). The commission: "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost… and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world" (Matthew 28:19–20).

**Ascension.** "He led them out as far as to Bethany, and he lifted up his hands, and blessed them. And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them, and carried up into heaven" (Luke 24:50–51; Acts 1:9: "a cloud received him out of their sight").

**Acts.** Peter at Pentecost: "God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ" (Acts 2:36), and "there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved" (4:12, 0.72).

**In the letters.** Paul's core: Jesus was "declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead" (Romans 1:4); "God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us" (5:8); "if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved" (10:9); "Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; and… he was buried, and… he rose again the third day" (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). Philippians 2:6–8: "being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: but made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant… became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross". Colossians 1:15: "the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature". Hebrews 1:3: "the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power"; 4:15: "in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin"; 13:8: "Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever."

**Revelation.** "I am he that liveth, and was dead; and, behold, I am alive for evermore, Amen; and have the keys of hell and of death" (1:18). "Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing" (5:12). "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS" (19:16). The book ends "Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus" (22:20).

**Old Testament links.** The classifier links all fifteen verses of Isaiah 52:13–53:12, the Suffering Servant passage, at 0.68–0.94 (Isaiah 53:3–12 are all at 0.89 or above), plus Psalm 2, parts of Psalm 22 (22:16 at 0.66; 22:1 fell just under at 0.48) and Psalm 110, Isaiah 9:6–7, 11, 42 and 49, Micah 5:2–3, Zechariah 9:9–10, Daniel 9:25–26, Jeremiah 23:6 and Malachi 3:2. Example: "He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief" (Isaiah 53:3); "he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities" (53:5); "Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee" (Psalm 2:7); "Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek" (110:4); "thou Bethlehem Ephratah… out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel" (Micah 5:2); "thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass" (Zechariah 9:9). See "Reading the data".

## Relationships

- **[Mary](mary_mother.md)** (mother, 53 shared verses) and **[Joseph](joseph_husband.md)** (legal father, 26). Brothers **James, Joses, Simon and Jude** (Matthew 13:55). Cousin/forerunner: **[John the Baptist](john_baptist.md)** (83).
- **Ancestry.** Matthew and Luke trace him through **[David](david.md)** (37 shared verses), **[Abraham](abraham.md)** (9), **[Solomon](solomon.md)** and, in Matthew 1, **[Rahab](rahab.md)**, **[Ruth](ruth.md)** and **[Bathsheba](bathsheba.md)**.
- **The twelve:** **[Peter](peter.md)**, **[John](john_apostle.md)**, **[Thomas](thomas.md)**, **[Judas Iscariot](judas_iscariot.md)**, and the other apostles (Andrew, James, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot).
- **Friends:** **[Lazarus](lazarus.md)**, Martha and Mary of Bethany; **[Mary Magdalene](mary_magdalene.md)** and the women who followed him.
- **Opponents and judges:** the chief priests, **[Pilate](pontius_pilate.md)** and Herod Antipas.
- **The prophets:** **[Moses](moses.md)** (29), **[Elijah](elijah.md)** (19), **[Isaiah](isaiah.md)**, **[Jonah](jonah.md)** (the "sign of Jonas"), **[Daniel](daniel.md)**, **[Jeremiah](jeremiah.md)**, **[Solomon](solomon.md)** ("a greater than Solomon"). He is compared with **[Adam](adam.md)** ("the last Adam", 8 shared verses in Romans 5 and 1 Corinthians 15), and appears in verses with **[Jacob](jacob.md)** (5) and **[Jezebel](jezebel.md)** (3, the speaker of the Thyatira letter in Revelation 2:20–22). "As it was in the days of Noe" (Matthew 24:37) is a Jesus verse, though not a shared one with [Noah](noah.md).

## Reading the data

- **The name and the titles.** TIPNR counts "Christ", "Lord", "Immanuel" and "Messiah" as forms of the name, so many of the links are verses that say only "Christ" or "the Lord" (most of Paul's letters); "Christian" is excluded.
- **Old Testament links are interpretive.** Isaiah 53, Psalm 22 and the other prophecy verses are linked because the classifier reads them as speaking of him, following the New Testament's own use. They carry no name in the Old Testament text. The same Old Testament passages are not linked to Isaiah or David.
- **Not linked where he is not the subject.** Parable characters (the father, the good Samaritan), verses spoken *about* him by crowds without a pronoun, and most lists of the twelve fall below 0.5.
- **Group scenes.** 1,453 links sit between 0.5 and 0.9, mostly verses in which "he", "they" or "the Lord" continue a scene; a few are weaker readings such as Acts 4:12 (0.72), John 1:1 (0.79) and Matthew 6:9 (0.69).
- 1,157 verses name him in TIPNR; the classifier's list is about 3.5 times that number because it also includes verses with no name.
