## Who Abel is

Abel is the least-linked character in the index (8 verses), because he is a victim rather than a speaker: in Genesis 4 he never says a word. What the Bible says about him is short and is echoed in the New Testament.

## What the verses tell us

- **Shepherd.** Eve "again bare his brother Abel. And Abel was a keeper of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground" (Genesis 4:2).
- **Accepted offering.** "Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof. And the LORD had respect unto Abel and to his offering" (4:4). Hebrews 11:4 gives the reason: "By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, by which he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying of his gifts: and by it he being dead yet speaketh."
- **Killed.** "Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him" (4:8). God asks Cain "Where is Abel thy brother?" (4:9) and says "the voice of thy brother's blood crieth unto me from the ground" (4:10); the earth "opened her mouth to receive thy brother's blood" (4:11).
- **Why, according to the New Testament.** 1 John 3:12: Cain slew him "Because his own works were evil, and his brother's righteous."
- **Memory.** Jesus lists "the blood of righteous Abel" as the first in a line of murdered prophets (Matthew 23:35; Luke 11:51). Hebrews 12:24 says Jesus's blood "speaketh better things than that of Abel". Eve names Seth "instead of Abel, whom Cain slew" (Genesis 4:25). None of these four verses reached the 0.5 threshold, because Abel is only mentioned in them, not acting.

## Relationships

- **[Adam](adam.md) and [Eve](eve.md)**: parents.
- **[Cain](cain.md)**: older brother and killer. Seven of Abel's eight linked verses are also linked to Cain.
- **Seth**: the son born to replace him (Genesis 4:25).
- No wife or children are recorded for him.

## Reading the data

Abel is only clearly present in Genesis 4:2, 4:4, 4:8–11, so the two New Testament links (Hebrews 11:4 at high probability; 1 John 3:12 at 0.59) are the only ones outside that scene. The 8 links are all correct; the misses are the four named-but-not-linked verses above.
