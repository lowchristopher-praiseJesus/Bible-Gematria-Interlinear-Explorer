## Who Noah is

Noah is the ninth man from Adam in the line of Seth (Genesis 5) and the link between the old world and the new. 68 of his 75 linked verses are in Genesis 5–10; the rest are later uses of his name as an example of faith, righteousness or "the days that were before the flood".

## What the verses tell us

**Birth and name.** His father Lamech "called his name Noah, saying, This same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands, because of the ground which the LORD hath cursed" (Genesis 5:29). At 500 he "begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth" (5:32; also 6:10).

**Character.** "Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD" (6:8); "Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God" (6:9). God later says "thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation" (7:1).

**The command and the ark.** God says "the end of all flesh is come before me; for the earth is filled with violence" (6:13) and gives the design: gopher wood, pitch inside and out, 300 × 50 × 30 cubits, a window, a side door, three stories (6:14–16). God will "establish my covenant" with him and bring in his wife, sons and their wives (6:18), plus two of every kind (6:19–20). The verdict on his obedience is repeated: "Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he" (6:22; 7:5).

**The Flood.** Noah is 600 when the waters come (7:6). He enters with his family and the animals (7:7, 7:13), "and the LORD shut him in" (7:16). "Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark" (7:23).

**After the water.** "God remembered Noah" (8:1). Noah opens the window, sends out a raven and then a dove three times; the dove returns "in the evening; and, lo, in her mouth was an olive leaf" (8:6–12). At God's word he leaves the ark (8:15–18) and builds an altar and offers burnt offerings of clean animals (8:20).

**The covenant.** God blesses Noah and his sons: "Be fruitful, and multiply" (9:1), gives animals for food but forbids blood (9:3–4), and requires an account for human life (9:5–6). The covenant is with Noah, his descendants "and with every living creature": "neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth" (9:9–11). The rainbow is the token (9:12–17).

**The vineyard.** "Noah began to be an husbandman, and he planted a vineyard: And he drank of the wine, and was drunken; and he was uncovered within his tent" (9:20–21). Ham sees and tells his brothers; Shem and Japheth cover their father walking backward (9:22–23). Noah wakes and curses Ham's son: "Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren" and blesses "the LORD God of Shem" (9:24–26).

**Death.** "Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years. And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years: and he died" (9:28–29).

**Later references.**
- *Ezekiel 14:14, 20:* "Noah, Daniel, and Job" are the three types of the righteous man, who could "deliver but their own souls by their righteousness". (This is why Noah shares two verses with [Daniel](daniel.md) and [Job](job.md).)
- *Matthew 24:38 and Luke 17:27:* Jesus compares the days before his return to "the days that were before the flood… until the day that Noe entered into the ark".
- *Hebrews 11:7:* "By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet… prepared an ark to the saving of his house".
- *1 Peter 3:20:* "the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah… wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water".
- *2 Peter 2:5:* God "saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness".

## Relationships

- **Lamech** (father) and, through him, **Methuselah** (grandfather, Genesis 5:25–28). His wife is never named in the text; TIPNR tentatively identifies her with Naamah (Genesis 4:22), marked uncertain there.
- **Shem, Ham and Japheth**: his three sons (5:32, 9:18). Ham's son Canaan is the one cursed (9:25). "Of them was the whole earth overspread" (9:19).
- **God**: he speaks to Noah directly at every turn in Genesis 6–9, and makes the covenant with him.

## Reading the data

- Some Flood verses carry Noah only by implication ("they went in", "the LORD shut him in"); links at 0.50–0.70 (Genesis 7:4, 7:14, 9:5, 9:18) are of that kind.
- The classifier missed a handful of named verses (below 0.5): 1 Chronicles 1:4, Genesis 10:32, Matthew 24:37, Luke 17:26 ("the days of Noe"), Luke 3:36 and Isaiah 54:9 ("the waters of Noah").
