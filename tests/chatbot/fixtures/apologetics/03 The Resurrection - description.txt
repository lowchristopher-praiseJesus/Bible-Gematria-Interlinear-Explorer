TITLE:
Nobody Dies for a Lie They Invented ⛓️ #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
A Roman magistrate brags he can make any liar confess before sundown. A counterfeiter takes one look at the whip and spills everything. 💰

Then they drag in a group of fishermen who claim their executed teacher is ALIVE: that they touched him and ate breakfast with him on the shore.

"Fanatics," he smirks. "People die for crazy beliefs all the time."

But there's a difference:
🔥 A fanatic dies for something someone else told him. He sincerely believes it.
👁️ The apostles weren't repeating a rumor. They claimed they saw it with their own eyes. If it was a lie, they'd KNOW, because they'd be the ones who made it up.

Offered freedom if they'd just say "it never happened," they were beaten, jailed… and sang in their chains.
⚔️ James, by the sword. Peter, in Rome. James, the brother of Jesus, stoned in Jerusalem. Not one of them is ever recorded taking it back.

People will die for a lie they believe. Nobody dies for a lie they invented.

Could you hold out if you knew it was a lie? 👇

📚 Further reading: Acts 5 & 12 · Acts 16:25 · 1 Clement 5 · Josephus, Antiquities 20.9.1 · John 21
(The magistrate is a fictional character representing the Roman authorities.)
