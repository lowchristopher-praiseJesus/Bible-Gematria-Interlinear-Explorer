TITLE:
He Trusted the Wrong Book 📜 #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
A professor calls the Gospels "legends" because they were written decades after Jesus… then holds up his favorite biography of Alexander the Great as "real history."

One student checked the dates. 👀

🏛️ Alexander the Great died in 323 BC. Our earliest surviving history of him (Diodorus) came nearly 300 years later, and the one historians trust most (Arrian) came 400+ years later.
✝️ Paul's letters were written within 20–30 years of the cross, quoting a resurrection creed many scholars date to within a few years of the events. Mark followed within about 40 years, and all four Gospels within a single lifetime, while eyewitnesses were still alive.

If 400 years is good enough for Alexander… 40 is good enough for Jesus.
